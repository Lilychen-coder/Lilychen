Big Mac index: which currencies look over- or undervalued?

Project 1

The question

In July 2026, how much more or less did a Big Mac cost in each country than in the US, once converted at market exchange rates?

The chart

Open the chart in a new tab

What I found

In July 2026, a Big Mac cost 45% more in Switzerland than in the US, but 62% less in Indonesia. 41 of the other 53 countries and areas had a cheaper Big Mac than the US once converted to dollars.
