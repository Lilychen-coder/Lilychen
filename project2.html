Network of Thrones: who matters, and by which measure?

In A Storm of Swords, which characters matter most in the network, and does the answer change with the measure you use?

The chart

Open the chart in a new tab (it needs an internet connection)

What I found

Jon has the highest brokerage score (0.230): he sits on more of the shortest routes between other characters than anyone else. Yet he ranks only 15th on eigenvector centrality, so being the main bridge is not the same as being linked to the other most central characters.

Use the buttons to switch measure, and the slider under the network to keep only characters with at least a chosen number of links.
